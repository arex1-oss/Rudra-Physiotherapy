Physiotherapy & rehabilitation

How we may be able to help

People enquire about physiotherapy for a range of pain, movement and recovery concerns. Contact the clinic to discuss your individual situation and suitability.

Back & neck discomfort

Support for stiffness, movement restriction and common back or neck pain concerns.

Knee, foot & ankle pain

Movement and mobility concerns that can affect walking, standing and daily activities.

Joint & mobility care

Help with stiffness, restricted movement and rebuilding confidence with everyday motion.

Injury recovery

Rehabilitation support after accidents, sprains and other injuries, based on individual needs.

Post-surgery rehabilitation

Guided recovery support after surgery, following the treating doctor's advice and clearance.

Neuro & stroke rehabilitation

Rehabilitation support for mobility and functional movement after neurological conditions.
